# Mise Privacy Policy

Mise ("the app") helps you keep a pantry and find dishes to cook. This policy explains what information the app handles.

## The short version
- Mise has **no accounts and no sign-in**.
- Your pantry, shopping list, saved and favourite dishes, equipment, and optional profile stay **on your device**. We do not receive them.
- We do not show ads and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

## Information stored on your device
The app stores the following locally on your phone: pantry items and quantities, shopping list, saved and favourite dishes, your equipment, and your optional profile (name, cuisine and flavour preferences, and a profile photo if you choose one). A profile photo you pick from your gallery is used only inside the app and is not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app, or using **Profile → Reset local app data**, removes this information. Reset does not cancel a subscription; that is managed in Google Play.

## Purchases
Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by **Google Play**. We use **RevenueCat** (revenuecat.com) to check which plan you have. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase information (such as which plan you bought and when). We never see your payment card details. RevenueCat's own privacy policy applies to the data it handles.

## Notifications
If you allow notifications, the app schedules **local** reminders on your device shortly before your subscription term ends. These are created on your phone; no notification service receives your data. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

## Images
Dish photos are loaded over the internet from image providers when you use the app. Those providers may see your device's IP address, as with any web image.

## Children
Mise is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

## Your choices
You can delete all app data by resetting it in the app or uninstalling the app. To manage or cancel a subscription, use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
